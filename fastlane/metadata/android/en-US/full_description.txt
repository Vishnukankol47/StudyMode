StudyMode is a privacy-first, 100% on-device digital wellbeing and anti-doomscrolling companion for Android.

Key Features:
• Live Floating Shorts & Reels Counter: A discreet, draggable floating pill over YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels that counts your swipes and live time spent.
• Intentional Friction: Encourages mindful consumption without frustrating lockouts.
• Zero-Tolerance Micro-Blockers: Option to block short-form video tabs instantly while keeping long-form study and tutorial content accessible.
• Focus & Exam Timers: Pomodoro and deep work timers to protect your study sessions.
• 100% On-Device & Privacy Guaranteed: Zero network permissions, zero tracking, zero telemetry, and zero ads.
• Strict Banking & UPI Isolation: Automatically ignores banking and UPI payment apps to guarantee complete safety and privacy.
