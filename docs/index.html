🛡️ Permissions & Privacy Transparency

Why does StudyMode need these permissions? We believe in 100% open-source transparency:

Permission What It Does What It CANNOT Do
Accessibility Detects when you open the Shorts/Reels tab to display the live counter pill. Zero keystroke or chat access. Hardcoded to completely disconnect from all banking apps.
Display Over Apps Floats the compact draggable HUD pill over your screen. Cannot draw over secure system windows (PIN keyboards, lockscreens).
Usage Access Computes today's screen time and daily limits on-device. All stats stay 100% inside your phone's local SQLite database.
Do Not Disturb Silences notification pings automatically during study sessions. Never reads or stores notification messages.
Cryptographic Guarantee: StudyMode contains zero analytics SDKs, zero ad trackers, and no remote data servers. Every single line of code is open on GitHub.
